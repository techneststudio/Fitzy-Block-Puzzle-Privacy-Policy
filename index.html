Fitzy Block Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Developer: TechNest Studio  |  Package ID: com.techneststudio.fitzypuzzle  |  Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Data Safety Overview Fitzy Block Puzzle stores your scores, streaks, levels, and settings locally on your device. Optional cloud sync uses Google Play Games Services, optional subscriptions/purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing, and free gameplay displays ads via Google AdMob. We never request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, location, or external storage permissions.

1. Introduction

TechNest Studio ("we", "our", or "us") provides the mobile game Fitzy Block Puzzle (available on Google Play). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, used, and protected when you play Fitzy Block Puzzle.

By installing or using Fitzy Block Puzzle, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Fitzy Block Puzzle saves your gameplay data locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore so your progress persists across sessions:

  • High scores per mode (Classic, Daily Board, Time Rush, Zen Sanctuary, Puzzle Levels, Versus).
  • Daily Board history, active streak count, longest streak, and streak freezes.
  • Unlocked puzzle levels, stars earned, achievements, and sound/vibration settings.
  • Entitlement status flags (Fitzy Plus active / Ads Removed) synchronized with Google Play Billing.

When you use the Share Score Card feature, the app renders a PNG score card image inside its private temporary cache directory and shares it via Android FileProvider strictly to the target app you select.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Processing

To deliver ads, optional purchases, and optional cloud backup, Fitzy Block Puzzle integrates official Google SDKs governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

Service / SDK Purpose Data Potentially Processed
Google AdMob
(Google Privacy Policy)		 Displays Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded video ads in free mode. Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse IP-derived location, app/ad interactions, crash & performance diagnostics.
Google Play Billing
(Play Terms)		 Processes Fitzy Plus subscriptions (Monthly/Yearly) and Remove Ads Only one-time purchases. Purchase tokens, product IDs, and subscription entitlement status. Payment card details are handled exclusively by Google Play.
Google Play Games Services v2
(Google Privacy Policy)		 Optional Cloud Save & Backup (SnapshotsClient) when enabled and signed in. Your encrypted Fitzy progress snapshot stored inside your personal Google Drive App Data folder tied to your Play Games profile.

4. Permissions Used by the App

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for loading ads, verifying Google Play Billing entitlements, and optional Google Play Saved Games sync.
  • VIBRATE: Used exclusively for optional tactile haptic feedback during block placement and line clears (can be disabled anytime in in-game Settings).

Fitzy Block Puzzle does not access or request location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone state, calendar, or broad media/external storage permissions.

5. Removing Ads & Managing Purchases

Purchasing Remove Ads Only or subscribing to Fitzy Plus disables Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded ad prompts within the app. You can manage or cancel active subscriptions at any time via the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.

6. Children's Privacy

Fitzy Block Puzzle does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

  • Delete Local Data: Clear app storage via Android Settings → Apps → Fitzy Block Puzzle → Storage → Clear Data or uninstall the app.
  • Opt Out / Reset Advertising ID: Manage ad personalization via Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Delete Cloud Saved Games Data: Manage or remove Play Games app data in your Google Account / Play Games settings, or contact us for assistance.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new gameplay features, SDK updates, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published at this page and reflected in the in-app Privacy Policy screen with an updated effective date.
